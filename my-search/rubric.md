# My job scoring rubric

> Replace everything below with your own rubric. The easiest way is to run the
> [Level 1 starter prompt](../prompts/1-starter.md) and paste the rubric it gives you at the end.
> The example below shows the shape it should take. Delete the example when you're done.

## About my search

- **Job titles I'm looking for:** e.g. Programme Manager, AI Enablement Lead, Customer Success Manager
- **Where I'm based:** e.g. Bedford, UK
- **Location and working pattern:** e.g. fully remote, or hybrid in London with no more than 2 office days a week
- **Minimum salary:** e.g. £60,000 (ideal £75,000+)
- **Country to search in:** e.g. United Kingdom

## Scoring (out of 100)

| Section | Points | Full points if... | Low points if... |
|---|---|---|---|
| The work itself | 25 | Teaching, enabling and running programmes that help people adopt new tools | Mainly sales targets, or writing production software |
| Type of role | 20 | Programme management, enablement, customer success | Account management, pure sales |
| Requirements I can honestly meet | 20 | I meet most essential requirements from my CV | Needs deep skills I don't have (e.g. 5+ years of software engineering) |
| Level, salary and location | 20 | At or above my level and minimum salary, remote or London hybrid | Below my minimum, or 3+ office days a long way from home |
| Energy and growth | 15 | New skills, working with smart people, visible impact | Repetitive work, no growth path |

## Thresholds

- **75 or more:** apply
- **60 to 74:** stretch, worth a closer look
- **Below 60:** skip

## Deal-breakers (any of these = score 0)

- e.g. Fully office-based outside London
- e.g. Commission-only pay
- e.g. Gambling or tobacco industries
